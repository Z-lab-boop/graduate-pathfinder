Profile

Start with the right context.

Name the intake year, applicant route and academic background. A requirement only matters when it applies to your case.

Context before comparison

02

Materials

Read beyond the checklist.

Review resumes, transcripts and recommendation drafts. Keep extraction uncertainty, missing signatures and target-specific gaps visible.

A file exists ≠ a material is ready

03

Targets

Compare claims, not reputations.

Separate eligibility, fit, material readiness and evidence confidence for each target. Keep every requirement connected to its source.

One target, several independent dimensions

04

History

Give every count a scope.

Read previous-cycle interview timelines and stage counts with their year, route, numerator and denominator. Incompatible scopes stay unresolved.

Past cohort ratios are not personal probabilities

05

Outlook

Make uncertainty part of the decision.

Bring the evidence together into an ordinal outlook. Surface the next review step alongside missing and conflicting information.
