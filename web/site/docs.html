Three versions, separate decisions

v0.1 · Baoyan: screen opportunities, compare specified targets, build a portfolio, and check deadlines and schedule conflicts. Expected recommendation eligibility remains separate from confirmed eligibility.

v0.2 · Kaoyan: check the complete subject combination, score scenarios, distinct threshold meanings, risk bands and shortlists. Baoyan groups do not replace kaoyan decisions.

v0.3 · Materials and outlook: review local materials against target requirements, reconstruct scoped previous-cycle timelines and aggregate counts, and explain eligibility, packet readiness, ordinal competition, fit and evidence confidence independently.

Install the complete Skill

Install the whole repository directory: SKILL.md, references/, scripts/ and agents/ are all required. You may also copy the complete repository into the Codex skills directory. Keep case files outside the installed Skill.

Clone the repository from its Code menu, then run this macOS/Linux command from the repository root. It refuses to overwrite an existing destination and validates the installed Skill.

set -eu
codex_root="${CODEX_HOME:-$HOME/.codex}"
skill_parent="$codex_root/skills"
skill_target="$skill_parent/graduate-pathfinder"
mkdir -p "$skill_parent"
if [ -e "$skill_target" ] || [ -L "$skill_target" ]; then
  printf '%s\n' "Refusing to overwrite existing skill target: $skill_target" >&2
  exit 1
else
  ln -s "$(pwd)" "$skill_target"
  python3 "$skill_parent/.system/skill-creator/scripts/quick_validate.py" "$skill_target"
fi

Local cases and review deliverables

A quick screen may stay in chat. Create a case only when persistence or file delivery is requested, outside the installed Skill, normally at graduate-pathfinder-cases/<anonymous-label>/ in the working directory. Originals stay at the selected local paths; a manifest records paths, hashes and extraction states without copying or rewriting them.

material-audit.md
Target-specific completeness, missing conditions, contradictions and review flags; completeness does not authenticate documents or mean submission.
applicant-claims.json
Normalized academic claims with six evidence states and source-document IDs. Keep missing values and conflicting claims; omit private identifiers.
historical-cycle.json
Explicit historical scope, timelines, aggregate stage counts, calculations and all refusal reasons; no applicant list rows.
admission-outlook.md
Five independent results, uncertainty, missing evidence and verifiable conditions that could change an ordinal judgment.
privacy-review.md
Redaction findings, possible over-redaction, missed-information checks and an honest pending/completed human-review status.

Private manifests, material requirements and full evidence audits remain local review inputs. Public-safe deliverables still need redaction and human privacy review before sharing; automated checks do not guarantee anonymity.

Evidence and privacy boundaries

Check current requirements, dates and counts against official evidence for the exact intake year and target identity: institution, unit, program code, degree, research track and admission round. Unknown, stale or conflicting facts stay unresolved.

T1 current official notices support current critical requirements. T2 official clarification supports checks; T3/T4 discovery sources do not replace T1. A–D audit confidence is separate from source tier and year applicability. Source labels alone do not verify a webpage.

Material review does not authenticate documents. A visible file, a hash, OCR text or a resume statement does not establish authenticity. Missing signatures or dates, unreadable pages, contradictions and unclear policies remain review blockers.

Historical ratios are not personal probabilities. Only matching year, institution, unit, program code, degree, track and round with a verified denominator can support final_admitted / confirmed_attended. Listed or deduplicated names are not confirmed attendance; incompatible or ambiguous counts are refused. Past dates do not establish current deadlines.

Competitive tiers are ordinal: strong, competitive, borderline, weak, evidence_insufficient. They remain separate from baoyan portfolio groups and kaoyan risk bands. Missing current competitive evidence or comparable history stays evidence_insufficient; no tier guarantees admission.

This site uses fictional demo data, accepts no uploads and has no tracking. The local-first Skill keeps originals local and does not submit applications, contact supervisors, send messages or accept offers. Remove private identifiers from generated reports and review redaction manually before sharing.

Public source, tests and release status

Graduate Pathfinder is a local-first Codex Skill. Public source and tests are available in the repository. These checks cover structure, logic and synthetic behavior; they do not authenticate materials or prove current nationwide admissions coverage.
